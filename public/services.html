02 / Web apps & PWAs

A better tool
for the work.

Turn a manual process or product idea into a web application people can use in a browser. We’ll agree on the essential workflows and build a maintainable first version around them. A progressive web app (PWA) can also be added to a phone’s home screen, where supported, without a separate native app.

Explore web app pricing → See Nabu & Year of Bingo in action → Talk about a web application →
  • Planning, screen design, and working prototypes
  • Responsive browser tools and installable PWAs
  • Customer tools and internal business apps
  • Accounts, payments, and connections where needed

Two useful starting points. Nabu shows shared task logging in an installable PWA. Year of Bingo shows a visual goal board and progress tracking in a browser app. A business might need a shared job log or a customer progress tracker. We would scope that workflow first; recreating either complete product requires a larger custom estimate.
