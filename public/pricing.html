Before you decide

A few things
you might be wondering.

Have a different question? Email us and we’ll talk it through.

What does a care plan cover?

Care plans cover agreed updates, fixes, and monitoring after launch. The scope and monthly cost are set out in your proposal. We’ll explain the options so you can choose the support that fits.

Do I need all my content ready?

No. Start with what you have: your current site, a menu, some photos, or just an idea. We’ll work out what’s needed and who will provide it as part of the plan.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size of the project and how quickly content and feedback are ready. We’ll agree on a realistic timeline with you before work starts.

Can you work on my existing website or app?

Tell us what you have and what you’d like to change. We’ll look at how it was built and discuss whether an update or a new build makes more sense.

What happens if I need something extra?

We’ll discuss the extra work and any effect on price or timing with you before adding it to the project.
