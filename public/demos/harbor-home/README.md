# Harbor Home — Business Website example

Harbor Home is a fictional home-maintenance business concept. The pages now use a straightforward service-company structure: a clear maintenance headline and estimate action, a recognizable task directory, an explanation of scope and next steps, service limits, and a short local inquiry preview. The oak entry image is an AI-generated concept visual, not a customer property or completed job.

## Pages

- `index.html` — home repair and routine-upkeep overview with task links and estimate action
- `services.html` — common repair, fitting, and seasonal-upkeep tasks with clear exclusions
- `about.html` — working approach and service-coverage inquiry
- `faq.html` — practical answers about scope, coverage, estimates, and the form
- `contact.html` — native-validated local inquiry preview

The five pages use responsive HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. The only form response is rendered in the current browser page; the demo does not submit, store, or send entered details. With JavaScript disabled, the request button remains disabled and the explanatory note stays visible. Main navigation remains available without JavaScript.

## Design references

These public company sites informed the information architecture only; no copy, images, claims, or business details were reused:

- [Johnny’s Handyman Services](https://jhservices.us/) — task categories, a direct quote path, compact request form, service-area checks, and practical FAQs.
- [All In One Maintenance](https://allinonemaint.com/) — grouped home-repair services, visible estimate path, service-area context, and straightforward inquiry structure.

All business details shown in this example are fictional. It makes no claims about real availability, addresses, credentials, ratings, results, or customers. Use `/demos/harbor-home/index.html` in the repository's existing preview server; no dependencies or build step are required.
