Home repairs

Everyday repairs

Small repairs and adjustments that make rooms easier to use and help keep a home in good condition.

  • Door alignment and hardware adjustments
  • Minor drywall patches and paint touch-ups
  • Trim, cabinet hardware, and drawer adjustments
  • Picture and shelf mounting on suitable surfaces
Before work starts

The surface, access, and materials affect what is possible. We confirm the task and any preparation before scheduling.

Fixtures and fittings

Fixtures, fittings & assembly

Help with common household installations and furniture that needs careful assembly or adjustment.

  • Ready-to-assemble furniture
  • Curtain tracks and shelving
  • Cabinet pulls and door hardware
  • Caulking touch-ups in suitable areas
Service limits

We do not take on electrical, plumbing, structural, gas, or roof work. These jobs need a properly qualified trade.

Seasonal upkeep

Seasonal maintenance

Routine checks and small upkeep tasks that help homeowners stay ahead of ordinary wear through the year.

  • Weatherstripping and visible seals
  • Filter changes where access is safe
  • Accessible walk-around observations
  • Prioritize upkeep and note follow-up needs
What to expect

The scope is agreed before a visit. We flag items that need a specialist rather than expanding the job beyond its limits.

Work we do not take on

Know when a job
needs another trade.

The services shown here are routine home repairs and maintenance. Electrical, plumbing, gas, structural, roof, pest, and emergency work require a specialist or an appropriate emergency provider.

When a task is outside the agreed scope, the right next step is to contact a properly qualified trade for advice and service.
