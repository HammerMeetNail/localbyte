HOW TO USE THIS LIST

Ask about a task ↗
01PREVENTIVE

Seasonal home check

A focused walk-around of routine maintenance items, adjusted for the home and the time of year.

  • Visible exterior condition check
  • Door and window seals / latches
  • Filter and detector reminder list
  • Prioritized upkeep notes
GOOD TO KNOW

Access and safe working conditions are confirmed first. Roof work and specialist inspection are outside this example scope.

02REPAIR

Small fixes & adjustments

Take care of manageable tasks that interrupt the day or keep getting pushed down the list.

  • Simple hardware adjustment
  • Minor trim and fixture touch-ups
  • Basic assembly and mounting
  • Identify when a specialist is needed
WORK LIMITS

Electrical, plumbing, structural, and other regulated work would be referred to a qualified specialist.

03PLANNING

Home task list

Turn a handful of open jobs into an understandable order, with dependencies and next steps noted.

  • Organize tasks by urgency and season
  • Separate quick fixes from larger projects
  • Prepare clear notes for follow-up quotes
  • Pick a sensible first task
PLANNING ONLY

The list is a conversation and agreed scope, not a booking calendar or a promise to perform every item.

SCOPE / RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

Clear limits are
part of good service.

This fictional example is built around routine maintenance and small, non-regulated jobs. It makes no claim of trade licensing, inspection credentials, emergency response, or insurance.

For a real business, the confirmed service menu and applicable credentials would be discussed with the owner before this copy went live.
