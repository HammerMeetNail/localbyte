02 Services / a useful starting list

Keep the house
on your side.

A practical menu for small maintenance work. Each job begins with a conversation about what is needed, what is in scope, and who is the right person to do it.

Ask about a task ↗
01 SEASONAL

Seasonal home checks

Work through a focused list of routine upkeep as the weather changes. The exact checklist depends on the home, access, and the season.

  • Visible exterior walk-around
  • Door, latch, and window adjustments
  • Gutter and downspout visual check from safe access
  • Prepare and prioritize a maintenance list
Good to know

Inspection, repair, and specialist trade work are separate scopes. We confirm what can be handled before agreeing to a visit.

02 SMALL REPAIRS

Everyday fixes

Handle those manageable home tasks that interrupt your day or keep sliding down the list. Share a description and, if useful, a photo when you inquire.

  • Minor hardware and fixture adjustments
  • Small interior touch-ups and practical repairs
  • Assembly and installation of simple household items
  • Clear note when a licensed specialist is the better fit
Scope first

Some work requires a qualified or licensed trade. This concept does not claim credentials or offer regulated services.

03 PLANNING

Make a home task list

When there are several things to sort out, a little sequencing can help. Build a useful punch list, identify dependencies, and decide what to take on first.

  • Walk-through notes based on the agreed scope
  • Separate urgent, seasonal, and optional items
  • Plain-language descriptions for follow-up quotes
  • Next-step suggestions with no pressure to bundle work
Simple by design

Planning is a conversation, not a custom booking system. Reach out and agree the details directly.

A straightforward process
