Field notes / Garden concept

The idea

Useful spaces. Thoughtful planting.

The studio concept is built around the relationship between a landscape and daily life. It favours a clear plan, a small number of strong decisions, and planting that belongs to its setting.

“Start with how the garden should feel on an ordinary day.”

A design principle for this fictional concept, not a customer quotation.

Studio notes

The character behind the work.

The illustrations and garden briefs on this site are original sample compositions. They are a way to show an approach, without presenting fictional projects as client work.
