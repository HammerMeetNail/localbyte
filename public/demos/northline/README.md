# Northline Studio — Business Website example

Northline Studio is a fictional residential interior-design practice. Its pages present a commercial studio structure: direct Work, Services, Studio, and Contact navigation; concrete design deliverables and process; two distinct image-based concept studies; and a local inquiry preview. Quiet House and Kitchen Study are AI-generated visual concepts, not client commissions, built work, or measured results.

## Pages

- `index.html` — practice overview, estimate/contact actions, and paired concept studies
- `services.html` — spatial planning, material selection, furnishing direction, deliverables, and scope boundaries
- `work.html` — Quiet House and Kitchen Study concepts with design-intent notes
- `about.html` — practical approach to layouts, materials, and collaboration
- `contact.html` — native-validated local inquiry preview

The five pages use responsive HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. The form previews entered details in the current browser page and does not submit, store, or send them. With JavaScript disabled, the request button remains disabled and the explanatory note stays visible. Main navigation remains available without JavaScript.

## Design references

These public studio sites informed the information architecture only; no copy, images, project details, or business claims were reused:

- [Millward Architecture + Design](https://www.millwardarchitecturedesign.com/) — concise studio positioning, featured-project navigation, and a clear new-project inquiry route.
- [DeMare Studio](https://demarestudio.com/en/) — service paths, project context, a legible design process, and a practical first-contact action.

Use `/demos/northline/index.html` in the repository's existing preview server; no dependencies or build step are required.
