THE STUDIO / POINT OF VIEW04 — 05

A space is more
than its image.

Northline is a fictional interiors studio imagined around everyday use: the morning light, the clear route, the objects that make a room feel lived in.

A WAY OF WORKING

Observe what is already there.

Before choosing finishes or furniture, take stock of the light, the proportions, the daily routines, and what the space needs to hold. Design is a sequence of connected decisions, not a layer applied at the end.

Make each choice earn its place.

A useful plan leaves enough room for change and ordinary life. Materials, color, and objects can create character while keeping movement and function clear.
