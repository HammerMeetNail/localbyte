01 / SERVICES & PROCESS

Good design is a
series of clear choices.

Northline shapes a useful design direction around the way you live, the character of your space, and the decisions you need to make.

01

INTERIOR DIRECTION

Find the thread.

Start with how you want a room or home to feel and function. A clear design direction helps the next decisions connect—from layout and color to lighting and finish.

  • Project goals and priorities
  • Visual reference and mood direction
  • Room-by-room design notes
02

SPACE PLANNING

Make room
for the day.

Bring a little more intention to movement, furniture placement, and the relationship between areas. Plans are shaped to the information and measurements supplied for the agreed scope.

  • Furniture and circulation concepts
  • Simple annotated layout direction
  • Prioritized changes for a room refresh
03

MATERIAL & COLOR

Build a palette
with purpose.

Coordinate finishes, color, and texture into a direction that feels cohesive and grounded. Product sourcing and procurement would be scoped separately where needed.

  • Color and finish relationships
  • Material reference board
  • Lighting and detail considerations

A collaborative sequence

From first brief
to a considered plan.

The project scope determines how much detail is useful. Every engagement begins with clear questions and an agreed deliverable.

  1. 01
    Listen

    We discuss the space, your routines, and the constraints.

  2. 02
    Shape

    We develop a direction and a focused set of decisions.

  3. 03
    Refine

    We review the agreed work and make the planned revisions.

  4. 04
    Hand over

    You receive the agreed concepts and practical next steps.

PROJECT BOUNDARIES

This fictional studio does not claim construction management, architectural services, permitting, procurement, or contractor coordination. A real proposal would name deliverables and exclusions.
