Project process

From first brief to design handoff

The scope and deliverables are set out before work begins.

  1. ListenDiscuss the rooms, goals, reference images, budget range, and timing.
  2. ComposeReview available plans and develop a layout and design direction.
  3. RefinePresent the agreed drawings, material references, and options.
  4. Hand overConfirm selections and identify decisions for the project team.

Design packages describe interior design intent. Architecture, engineering, permit drawings, purchasing, and construction coordination are only included when specifically listed in a written proposal.
