Quiet House

An imagined interior study in late daylight, pale oak, and the quiet contrast of a low stone fireplace.

THE BRIEF / A FICTIONAL EXERCISE

How can a long living room connect its bright window edge with a quiet hearth? This study brings together a continuous oak wall, a low stone fireplace, and a central path through the seating area.

SPATIAL INTENT

One room.
Several ways to settle.

  1. 01Keep the main route through the room open.
  2. 02Balance the long oak wall with the low, linear hearth.
  3. 03Let a small number of materials set the atmosphere.
