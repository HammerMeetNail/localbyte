Quiet House

Spatial and material explorations used to show how an interior design direction can be developed and communicated.

Quiet House · Concept study

The living room concept brings a continuous oak wall and a low stone hearth into a long, naturally lit space. The layout keeps a clear route through the seating area and leaves room for different everyday uses.

Layout priorities

Keep the route between windows and seating open, preserve a focal view to the hearth, and group furnishings around conversation.

Material direction

Pale oak, limestone, warm linen, and charcoal accents create a restrained palette with clear contrast between surfaces.

Design intent

A room planned
for everyday use.

  1. CirculationKeep a clear path between the windows, seating, and hearth.
  2. Focal pointBalance the long storage wall with the low fireplace.
  3. MaterialsUse a limited palette of wood, stone, linen, and charcoal.
