02 / Spaces

Three spaces. One easy rhythm.

Move from a light-filled hall to a quieter room, with a courtyard in between.

An imagined setting / Original illustration

01 / The hall

A generous centre.

The hall is the heart of this imagined venue. A sequence of archways, a broad floor, and warm materials make a composed backdrop for a gathering.

Good for

A shared meal, a welcome, or a group discussion that benefits from everyone being in one place.

Planning notes

Table layout, movement, access needs, and supplier requirements belong in the first planning conversation. No capacity or availability is claimed by this demo.

02 / The courtyard

A change of scene.

An open-air pause can bring a different pace to a gathering. This courtyard concept creates a sheltered edge and a natural place to continue a conversation.

03 / The drawing room

A quieter corner.

A smaller room offers a starting point for focused work, a private conversation, or a gentle arrival before the main gathering.
