Through the growing year

The right work, at the right time.

A garden changes with the seasons. A care plan makes room for regular upkeep as well as the jobs that only come around once a year.

Spring

Clear winter debris, prepare planting areas, and make a plan for new growth.

Summer

Stay on top of weeds, watering, and the day-to-day care of beds and borders.

Fall

Collect leaves, review tired planting, and prepare the garden for the quieter months.

A straightforward approach

A clear plan before work begins.

Good garden care starts with a conversation about the space, the priorities, and the amount of help you need.

    Tell us about the garden

    Agree on the work

  3. 3

    Keep care manageable

    Review the next seasonal jobs and any simple aftercare between visits.
