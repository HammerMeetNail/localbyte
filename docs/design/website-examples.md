## Research and application

Reviewed on October 8, 2026. These references informed content hierarchy and familiar interaction patterns; their text, photographs, logos, operating facts, prices, testimonials, and credentials were not copied.

The earlier saturated poster treatments have been removed. Heading scale, spacing, accent colors, and navigation now support the service information. No fabricated reviews, awards, licenses, staff biographies, operating addresses, or live contact details have been added as credibility signals.

No demo accepts real inquiries, bookings, or payments. Plus handoffs show where one agreed existing provider could fit; they do not implement a custom scheduling or booking system. Central pricing remains the source for amounts and scope.
