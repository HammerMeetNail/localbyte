# Website example design directions

These fictional sites show how a package can serve different businesses. Package depth is expressed through content structure and inquiry capability; every tier receives a distinct visual identity.

| Example | Customer journey | Visual system | Package depth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Juniper Yard | Scan neighborhood garden services and start a friendly conversation | Sunny poster, rounded display type, botanical stickers, seasonal color bands | One page, five sections, simple inquiry |
| Goodform | Understand bookkeeping deliverables and the working process before a short intake | Cobalt and white, precise sans type, monospace labels, ruled reports | One page, five sections, simple inquiry |
| Harbor Home | Choose a household task, check scope and common questions, then make a request | Navy/orange service desk, compact utility navigation, task directory and work-order details | Five pages and a straightforward contact path |
| Northline Studio | Explore a coherent interior study, its materials and spatial intent, then inquire | Quiet gallery, minimal studio rail, large concept photograph, restrained editorial captions | Five pages including a detailed portfolio study |
| Fieldwork | Explore landscape studies and planning guidance, then build a project brief | Technical field guide, graph-paper grid, uppercase masthead, annotated plans | Eight pages and a progressive conditional brief |
| Gatherwell | Explore event spaces and gathering ideas while shaping an event inquiry | Pink/cherry hospitality identity, expressive type, venue photography, capsule controls | Eight pages, filtered gallery and a live local planning summary |

No demo accepts real inquiries, bookings, or payments. Plus handoffs illustrate where one agreed existing provider could fit; they do not implement a custom scheduling or booking system. Central pricing remains the source for amounts and scope.

### Final prompt: Northline interior

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photograph for a fictional architectural interior studio website. Create one exceptionally composed, photoreal imagined living room: tactile warm oak wall joinery, low oatmeal linen sofa, a sculptural dark chair, pale mineral walls, large rectangular side windows, a restrained ceramic vessel and an open art book. Contemporary quiet architecture, authentic material texture, soft late-afternoon sidelight and long gentle shadows. Camera at seated height with straight architectural verticals, asymmetrical composition, broad horizontal 3:2 framing that also allows a center crop. Understated, calm, precise, premium editorial photography, not a sterile real-estate listing or a 3D-render aesthetic. The room is an entirely fictional concept. No people, no signage, no logos, no text, no watermarks, no arches, no ornate decor. Full-bleed image only; no webpage mockup or borders.
